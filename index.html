IBM 6540 Assignments

  • M02 – Introduction to Data Science in R Reflection
    Reflection on reproducible data science, Quarto, and the Tidyverse ecosystem.

  • W02 – Fundamentals of Machine Learning Reflection
    Reflection on tidymodels, machine learning concepts, model evaluation, and MLOps.

  • W03-1 – Regression Fundamentals and Chapter 5 Reflection
    Reflection on linear regression, R-squared, multiple regression, regression assumptions, and hands-on exercises using the mtcars and customer_data datasets.

  • W04-1 – Reflection on Logistic Regression and Ch8: Preprocessing with Recipes
    Reflection on logistic regression, odds, preprocessing recipes, and classification modeling.

  • M05 – Reflection on Odds, Log(Odds), Odds Ratio, and Log(OR)
    Reflection based on the Step 1 class recording covering logarithms, probability, odds, log odds, odds ratios, logistic regression foundations, and exam-ready R code patterns.

  • M05 – Visualizing Data with Tables
    Reflection on effective table design using the gt and gtExtras packages, including descriptive statistics, inline distribution graphics, professional table styling, and applications for marketing analytics reports.

  • M06-1 – Improving LADD Coffee's Digital Strategy
    RevealJS proposal presentation examining how LADD Coffee, a newly opened specialty coffee shop in West Covina, can strengthen its digital strategy through local search, social media, product-led content, first-party measurement, and customer journey analytics. The presentation includes reproducible R visualizations, a proposed channel-priority framework, customer journey analysis, measurement planning, data requirements, analytics methods, and a 90-day digital strategy.

  • M08 – Reflection on Regression-Focused Methods and Classification Models
    Machine learning reflection using the Retail Sales with Seasonal Trends and Marketing dataset, including a customer-oriented analytics objective, supervised regression, data splitting, preprocessing recipes, 10-fold cross-validation, linear regression, Ridge regression, Lasso regression, model comparison, test-set evaluation, and feature-importance analysis.
